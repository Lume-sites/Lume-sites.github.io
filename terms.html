Terms of Service

Last updated: September 1, 2026.

1. Scope of the service

These Terms govern the engagement of Lume to create institutional and presentation websites for small businesses and professionals. The purpose of the service is to organize business information, present products or services, and make it easier for potential customers to get in touch.

2. Lume Package and price

The Lume Package has the promotional price stated at the time of engagement. Under the offer currently advertised, the price is R$397.00.

Payment is split into two stages: 50% to start the project and 50% after final approval, before publishing on the final domain.

3. Scope

The package includes 1 complete website with up to 5 main sections or pages, adapted for mobile, tablet and desktop, plus setup and publishing.

When relevant to the project, the website may include features such as a WhatsApp button, Google Maps, social media links, photo gallery, simple contact form, opening hours, business presentation, services and frequently asked questions.

4. Delivery timeline

The standard delivery timeline is up to 7 business days and starts only after all materials required to carry out the project have been received.

If the client delays sending required materials, information, approvals or responses, the schedule will be extended accordingly.

5. Materials provided by the client

The client is responsible for providing, as required, logo, texts, business information, photos, contact details, address, opening hours, social media and visual references.

The client represents that they have sufficient authorization and rights to use the materials sent to Lume, including images, trademarks, texts and other content.

6. Revisions

Up to 2 revision rounds are included before publishing. One round means one consolidated submission of changes relating to the version presented.

Additional revisions, significant changes in direction after approval of a visual proposal, or requests made after publishing may be reviewed and charged separately.

7. Services not included

Unless expressly contracted separately, the Lume Package does not include:

  • online store, e-commerce, cart, checkout and online payments;
  • members area, user login or restricted content;
  • custom systems, dashboards, CRM, inventory control or databases;
  • complex scheduling and complex integrations with external systems;
  • logo or visual identity creation;
  • professional photo and video production;
  • extensive copywriting from scratch;
  • unlimited revisions or ongoing maintenance.

8. Domain

The first year of the domain is included in the package, subject to the conditions and extensions compatible with the offer presented by Lume.

The domain will be registered in the client’s name whenever permitted by the registrar and registration process. After the first year, renewal becomes the client’s responsibility and will follow the registrar’s price at that time.

9. Hosting and third-party services

Lume uses third-party infrastructure and services for publishing, domains and external features. The Lume Package does not include a monthly hosting fee charged by Lume.

Platforms such as hosting providers, domain registrars, WhatsApp, Google Maps and social networks have their own rules and may change features, policies, prices or availability independently of Lume. If an external change requires a significant adaptation or migration, Lume may propose a solution and a separate quote if applicable.

10. Approval and publishing

After the first version, the client may use the included revision rounds. Upon final approval, the second 50% payment becomes due. After payment is confirmed, Lume completes the final setup and publishes the website on the final domain.

11. Cancellation and refunds

  • before production begins: full refund of the amount paid;
  • after development has started and before the first version: 30% of the total project price is retained to compensate for work already performed;

  • after final approval or publishing: cancellation for simple change of mind is not available.

These rules do not limit mandatory rights granted by applicable consumer law, including any statutory cooling-off right or other legally required refund.

After full payment, the client will hold the rights to the content they provided, the domain registered in their name, and the website developed specifically for the project, subject to third-party components, libraries, tools and services governed by their own licenses and terms.

13. Lume portfolio

Lume may display the completed project in its portfolio, social media and promotional materials as an example of work performed. If the client does not want the project displayed, they may request its removal from the portfolio.

14. Client responsibilities

The client is responsible for the accuracy of published information, the rights to use submitted materials, and ensuring that their business content complies with rules applicable to their activity.

15. Communication

Project communications may take place via WhatsApp and email. The client must keep contact details up to date and respond to requests necessary for the work to continue.
