Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026.

1. About this Policy

This Policy explains in simple terms how Lume may collect, use, store and share personal data when a person contacts us, browses our website or purchases our services.

2. Data we may receive

Depending on how you contact us, we may receive information such as:

  • name;
  • phone and WhatsApp number;
  • email address;
  • business name and information;
  • messages and content sent for support or project development;
  • basic technical browsing and performance data when analytics tools are enabled.

3. How we use data

Data may be used to respond to enquiries, understand a potential client’s needs, prepare proposals, perform contracted services, communicate about the project, provide support, comply with legal obligations, and improve the website and customer service.

4. Legal bases for processing

We process personal data in accordance with applicable legal bases, which may include pre-contractual steps requested by the data subject, performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, legitimate interests, and consent where required.

5. Sharing with third parties

Lume does not sell personal data. Some data may be processed by providers required to operate the service, such as hosting providers, domain registrars, email services, WhatsApp, Google Maps, social networks and analytics tools.

These third parties have their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Analytics, cookies and similar technologies

Lume uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to monitor aggregated information about website visits and performance, such as pages viewed, loading time, browser, operating system, approximate country and technical experience metrics.

We also use Cloudflare Zaraz to record interaction events, such as clicks on WhatsApp buttons. These events may include the page where the click occurred and the position of the button used, in order to understand which parts of the website generate more enquiries.

Zaraz advanced reports may use cookies or session identifiers to organize browsing metrics, events, funnels and reports related to website usage. These features are used for analysis and website improvement, not for selling personal data or Lume behavioral advertising.

Cloudflare technologies are operated under Cloudflare’s own terms and policies. Where applicable, consent and cookie preference mechanisms may be used to allow visitors to control non-essential technologies.

7. Retention

Data will be retained for as long as necessary for customer service, negotiations, performance of services, support, defense of rights, business records or compliance with legal obligations. When there is no longer a need or legal basis for retention, data may be deleted or anonymized.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information under our responsibility against unauthorized access, alteration, loss or improper disclosure. However, no digital environment can be considered completely risk-free.

9. Data subject rights

Under applicable law, you may request information about the processing of your data and, where applicable, request access, correction, updating, deletion, anonymization, portability, information about sharing, or withdrawal of consent.

10. How to make a request

For matters related to privacy and your personal data, contact us by email contato@sitesdalume.com.br . We may request additional information to confirm a person’s identity before fulfilling certain requests.

11. External links and services

The website may contain links to WhatsApp, Google Maps, Instagram, client websites and other external services. When accessing these platforms, data processing is also subject to the rules and policies of the respective service.

12. Updates to this Policy

This Policy may be updated to reflect changes to the website, services used or applicable law. The current version will remain published on this page with the update date.

13. Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to contato@sitesdalume.com.br .
